# Motics
> AI-powered healthcare automation platform providing AI agents for clinical documentation, billing, and patient communication for clinics, hospitals, and care homes in the UK.

Motics uses AI Agents to automate administrative tasks for healthcare providers. Our mission is to help clinicians save 2+ hours per day on paperwork so they can focus on patient care. We serve NHS trusts, private clinics, hospitals, care homes, and telehealth providers across the United Kingdom.

## Core Products

### AI Agents
- [Scribe Agent](https://www.motics.ai/agents/scribe/): Automated clinical notes, letters, discharge summaries, and referral letters. Real-time documentation that saves clinicians 2+ hours daily.
- [Phone Agent](https://www.motics.ai/agents/phone/): 24/7 AI receptionist for appointment booking, patient calls, follow-ups, and out-of-hours triage. Reduces call burden by 90%.
- [Audit Agent](https://www.motics.ai/agents/audit/): Clinic-wide documentation review that audits 100% of clinical notes for quality, compliance, and CQC readiness.
- [Email Agent](https://www.motics.ai/agents/email/): Automated patient emails, follow-ups, and administrative communications with professional tone and timely responses.
- [Billing Agent](https://www.motics.ai/agents/billing/): Automated insurance claims, pre-authorisation, revenue cycle management, and billing compliance.
